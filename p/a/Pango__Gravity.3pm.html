NAME

Pango::Gravity - Constants for text gravity

HIERARCHY

Pango::Gravity

METHODS

No methods are available.

ENUMS AND FLAGS

Pango::Gravity

Represents the gravity of the text. The gravity determines how text is oriented within a line, and can be affected by the writing direction. This is particularly relevant for scripts like Arabic and Hebrew, where the shape of certain characters changes depending on their position within a word.

The possible values for Pango::Gravity are:

  • PANGO_GRAVITY_FORCE_LEFT_TO_RIGHT: Text flows from left to right.
  • PANGO_GRAVITY_FORCE_RIGHT_TO_LEFT: Text flows from right to left.
  • PANGO_GRAVITY_GRAH_LEFT_TO_RIGHT: Text flows from left to right, with gravity affecting the shape of characters.
  • PANGO_GRAVITY_GRAH_RIGHT_TO_LEFT: Text flows from right to left, with gravity affecting the shape of characters.
