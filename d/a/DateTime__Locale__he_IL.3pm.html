Patrones de Strftime

%c (%H:%M:%S %Y %b %d %a) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 18:30:30 2008 פבר׳ 05 יום ג׳
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 09:05:02 1995 דצמ׳ 22 יום ו׳
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 04:44:23 -10 ספט׳ 15 שבת

%x (%d/%m/%y) - formato de fecha

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 05/02/08
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 22/12/95
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 15/09/10

%X (%H:%M:%S) - formato de hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 18:30:30
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 09:05:02
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 04:44:23
