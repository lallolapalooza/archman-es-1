Patrones de Strftime

%c (%d %b, %Y %I:%M:%S %p) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 05 feb, 2008 06:30:30 PM
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 22 dic, 1995 09:05:02 AM
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 15 sep, -10 04:44:23 AM

%x (%d %b, %Y) - formato de fecha

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 05 feb, 2008
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 22 dic, 1995
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 15 sep, -10

%X (%Z %I:%M:%S %p) - formato de hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = UTC 06:30:30 PM
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = UTC 09:05:02 AM
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = UTC 04:44:23 AM
