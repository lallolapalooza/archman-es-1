Patrones de Strftime

%c (%a, %d %b %Y %T %Z) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = вто., 05 фев. 2008 18:30:30 UTC
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = пет., 22 дек. 1995 09:05:02 UTC
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = саб., 15 сеп. -10 04:44:23 UTC

%x (%d.%m.%Y) - formato de fecha

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 05.02.2008
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 22.12.1995
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 15.09.-10

%X (%T) - formato de hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 18:30:30
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 09:05:02
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 04:44:23
