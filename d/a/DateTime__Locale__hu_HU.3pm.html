Patrones de Strftime

%c (%Y. %b. %-e., %A, %H:%M:%S %Z) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 2008. feb.. %-e., martes, 18:30:30 UTC
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 1995. dic.. %-e., viernes, 09:05:02 UTC
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = -10. sept.. %-e., sábado, 04:44:23 UTC

%x (%Y-%m-%d) - formato de fecha

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 2008-02-05
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 1995-12-22
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = -10-09-15

%X (%H:%M:%S) - formato de hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 18:30:30
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 09:05:02
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 04:44:23
