Patrones de Strftime

%c (%y-%m-%d %T) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 08-02-05 18:30:30
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 95-12-22 09:05:02

 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = vie, 1995.eko abe.ren 22a
