Patrones de Strftime

%c (%A %d %b %Y %I:%M:%S %p) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = মঙ্গলবার 05 ফেব 2008 06:30:30 PM
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = শুক্রবার 22 ডিসে 1995 09:05:02 AM
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = শনিবার 15 সেপ -10 04:44:23 AM

%x (%-d/%-m/%y) - formato de fecha

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = %-d/%-m/08
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = %-d/%-m/95
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = %-d/%-m/10

%X (%I:%M:%S %p %Z) - formato de hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = 06:30:30 PM UTC
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 09:05:02 AM UTC
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 04:44:23 AM UTC
