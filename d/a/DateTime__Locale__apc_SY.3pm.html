Patrones de Strftime

%c (%a %b %e %H:%M:%S %Y) - formato de fecha y hora

 2008-02-05T18:30:30 = mar M02  5 18:30:30 2008
 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = vie M12 22 09:05:02 1995
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = sáb M09 15 04:44:23 -10

 1995-12-22T09:05:02 = 12/22/95
-0010-09-15T04:44:23 = 09/15/10
