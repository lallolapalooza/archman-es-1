Description

This node is used to apply a bump map or normal map to a surface. It works by modifying the surface normal during the rendering process. This gives the illusion of a bumpy surface without actually adding geometric complexity.

The bump map is specified either by a filename or by inline image data.

The wrapS and wrapT fields control how the texture coordinates are handled when they fall outside the [0,1] range.

The possible values for wrapS and wrapT are:

REPEAT
The texture is repeated. This is the default value.
CLAMP
The texture coordinates are clamped to the range [0,1].
EDGE
The edge texel is replicated to fill the space. This is equivalent to clamping in OpenGL.
