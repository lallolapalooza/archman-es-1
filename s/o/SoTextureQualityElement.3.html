Detailed Description

The SoTextureQualityElement is used to control the texture quality. This element affects the rendering of textures, specifically how they are sampled and filtered. Higher quality settings typically result in sharper and more detailed textures, but may come at the cost of performance. The allowed values are usually constants defined by the underlying graphics API or the Coin library itself.

This element is often used in conjunction with other texture-related elements, such as SoTexture2, SoTextureTransform, and SoTextureUnit.
