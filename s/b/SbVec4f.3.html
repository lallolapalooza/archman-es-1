Descripción detallada

La clase SbVec4f es un vector de 4 dimensiones con coordenadas de punto flotante.

Esta clase de vector es utilizada por muchas otras clases en Coin. Proporciona almacenamiento para un vector homogéneo de 3 dimensiones (con los 4 componentes usualmente referidos como <x, y, z, w>) así como operaciones aritméticas simples de punto flotante.

Description

This class represents a 4-dimensional vector. It has four components, conventionally named x, y, z, and w.

SbVec4f is used for representing homogeneous coordinates in 3D graphics. When used for homogeneous coordinates, the first three components (x, y, z) represent the Cartesian coordinates and the fourth component (w) is the homogeneous coordinate.

For example, in a perspective projection, a point (X, Y, Z) in 3D space is often represented as (X, Y, Z, 1) in homogeneous coordinates. After the projection is applied, the resulting point may be (X', Y', Z', W'), where W' may not be 1. To get back to 3D Cartesian coordinates, you divide the first three components by the fourth: (X'/W', Y'/W', Z'/W').

SbBool SbVec4f::equals (const SbVec4f & v, float tolerance) const

Compares the vector with v and returns TRUE if the distance between the vectors is smaller or equal to the square root of tolerance.

The comparison is done in 4D-space, i.e. the w component of the vector is not used to make x, y and z into Cartesian coordinates first.
